# Project Brief: Biko Shop Ordering and Inventory Management System

## 1. The problem
Small biko businesses often manage customer orders, product stocks, and payments manually and This makes it difficult to track transactions, monitor inventory, and organize business records. The system is built to make these tasks faster and more organized.

## 2. Users and what they need to do?
Customer | Browse biko products, place orders, and view order details.

Administrator | Manage products, categories, inventory, customers, employees, suppliers, purchases, orders, and payments.

Employee | View and process customer orders and update order statuses.

## 3. The database behind it?

biko_ordering_inventory
Number of tables: 11

Table:
Customer | Stores customer information and contact details.
Employee | Stores employee information and positions.
Category | Stores biko product categories.
Supplier | Stores supplier information.
Product | Stores product names, prices, and category references.
Order | Stores customer orders and their statuses.
Order_Detail | Stores the products and quantities included in each order.
Payment	| Stores payment dates, amounts, and methods.
Inventory | Tracks available product stocks.
Purchase  | Records purchases made from suppliers.
Purchase_Detail	| Stores the products and quantities included in each purchase.

## 4. Must-have features (for the final project)
1 Add customer orders and select biko products.
2 Manage products, categories, and prices.
3 Track product stocks and inventory levels.
4 Record customer payments and purchase transactions.
5 Update order statuses and customer information.
6 Generate reports for orders, sales, and inventory.

## 5. Nice-to-have features (only if there's time)
Generate printable receipts for completed orders.
